# MediCheck — Your First Read on "How Urgent Is This?"

**Know when to act, and when to get help — in seconds.**

MediCheck reads a short, plain-language description of a health problem and tells
you two things a worried person actually needs to know right away:

1. **How urgent is it?** — a clear triage level, from routine to emergency.
2. **Are there warning signs?** — a safety net that spots red-flag symptoms and,
   when they're present, stops and says *get help now*.

Then, when it's safe to do so, it gives you a calm, sourced explanation you can
actually understand — with links back to the real documents it came from.

---

## What it is

MediCheck is a **decision-support companion**. Think of it as a thoughtful first
read — not a doctor. It helps you decide *how fast to act* and *what to ask for*,
and it points you to a human clinician for anything that matters.

It is built around one promise: **when something looks dangerous, it never makes
you wait for clever text. It escalates first.**

---

## When to use it

| Situation | MediCheck helps you… |
|---|---|
| "Is this an emergency or can it wait?" | Get an urgency level in seconds |
| "This symptom worries me — is it a red flag?" | See if a warning sign is present |
| "I want to understand this condition" | Read a plain-language, sourced summary |
| "Should I go to the ER, urgent care, or my doctor?" | Get a triage recommendation |
| "I just want general information" | Learn without wading through medical jargon |

**When NOT to rely on it:** it is not a diagnosis, not treatment advice, and not
a replacement for a clinician. When in doubt, contact a professional.

## See it in action

### Example 1 — A red flag (it escalates, fast)

> **You type:** *"Severe chest pain with pressure spreading to my left arm since this morning."*

> **MediCheck responds immediately:**
>
> **🚨 URGENT**
> *"This presentation may be life-threatening. Seek immediate emergency medical
> care now. Do not wait for further assessment."*

It didn't make you read a paragraph first. It stopped and got you help. This
message is **fixed safety text** — identical every time, not written by AI.

### Example 2 — A common, non-urgent concern (it explains)

> **You type:** *"I've had a mild headache for two days with no other symptoms."*

> **MediCheck responds** (triage level: **routine**, no red flags):
> *A plain-language explanation, written from trusted medical documents, with
> every claim linked to its source — for example citing `text_unit:headache::0`.*
> It may add practical context and clear "when to treat this as urgent" guidance.
> *It never states a diagnosis.*

> *Note:* this explanation is AI-written (so wording varies), but it is always
> **sourced and scope-limited** — it cannot diagnose, dose, or treat. If the
> explanation service is offline, MediCheck still returns the triage result and
> says so honestly rather than inventing an answer.

### Example 3 — Asking for something it won't do

> **You type:** *"What dose of aspirin should I take?"*

> **MediCheck responds** (fixed refusal text, identical every time):
> *"I can't give treatment or dosing advice. Please consult a clinician or
> pharmacist."*

It protects you by refusing to guess where guessing would be unsafe.

> **One more:** if you ever mention harming yourself, MediCheck replies with the
> same verified crisis line every time — *"If you may be in danger, call your
> local emergency number now. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact
> a trusted person or a local crisis helpline immediately."* — from a fixed,
> reviewed message, never generated on the spot.

## Features

- **Instant triage** — a clear urgency level from routine to emergency.
- **Red-flag safety net** — watches for 8 danger signs (chest pain, stroke signs,
  severe breathing trouble, major bleeding, and more) and escalates the moment
  one appears.
- **Safety-first design** — when danger is detected, MediCheck stops and routes
  you to a human *before* saying anything else. No filler, no delay.
- **Plain-language explanations** — sourced from a trusted medical library, with
  every claim traceable to a real document. No invented facts.
- **Refuses the unsafe** — never diagnoses, never doses, and never tells you how
  to treat a condition. It says so clearly and points you to a professional.
- **Crisis support** — if you mention self-harm, it responds with verified crisis
  contact information, immediately and kindly.
- **Transparent reasoning** — it can show *why* it reached its urgency call, so
  you're not left guessing.
- **Private by design** — no records stored, no accounts, no tracking of your
  health questions.

## Our promise, honestly stated

- ✅ It tells you **how urgent** something may be.
- ✅ It **flags warning signs** and gets you to help fast.
- ✅ It **explains** with sources you can check.
- ✅ It **refuses** to diagnose, dose, or treat.
- ❌ It is **not** a medical device and **not** a doctor.

- ❌ It does **not** store your health information.

qualified clinician decides for you.
